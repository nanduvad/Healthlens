====================================================================
ANIMATION LIBRARIES
====================================================================

Use:

• Framer Motion
• GSAP (only where it adds value)
• Lenis (smooth scrolling)
• Lottie React (for loading and empty states)

Avoid excessive animations that reduce performance.

Maintain 60 FPS wherever possible.

====================================================================
PAGE TRANSITIONS
====================================================================

Every page transition should include:

• Fade
• Slide
• Scale

Use AnimatePresence from Framer Motion.

Transitions should feel smooth and premium.

Performance > 90

Accessibility > 95

Best Practices > 95

====================================================================
FINAL UI GOAL
====================================================================

The application should feel polished enough that judges immediately perceive it as a premium, production-quality AI healthcare platform.

Animations should enhance usability and storytelling, not distract from the experience. Prioritize smoothness, consistency, and responsiveness throughout the application.
